# Brand analysis template

One section per app in `design/inspiration/README.md`. Lead with the one-line idea that defines the brand, then cover each point that applies, citing screens and assets by file name.

```markdown
### <App>: <the one idea that carries the brand>
- **Mascot / characters:** who they are, what they *do* on screens (narrate, react, celebrate), how often they appear.
- **Illustration style:** flat / 3D / line, colour count, how consistent across screens.
- **Colour:** background(s), the primary action colour, what each colour means (mode, feedback, paid tier).
- **Shape and type:** button shape and depth, corner radius, type weight, caps or not.
- **Feedback:** right / wrong states, celebration moments, how mistakes come back.
- **Habit and reward:** streaks, XP, goals, progress bars, home layout.
- **Voice:** first person? playful? how onboarding questions are asked.
- **Chinese / Cantonese specifics:** romanisation placement and settings, tones, character size, audio.
```

## Takeaways for Canto

Each takeaway names the pattern, the apps that show it, and what it would mean in Canto concretely. Prefer ideas that fit a read-along video app: they should work around the transcript, not replace it.
