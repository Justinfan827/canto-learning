## Brand analysis

### Speak: a tech brand, not a cartoon: one blue, one geometric sans, and filmed humans
- **Mascot / characters:** **no mascot on the website.** The "characters" are **real filmed tutors**: actors at a table looking into the camera (`07-home-filmed-tutors.png`, `assets/png/video-still-filmed-tutor-*.png`), cast, according to a Speak job listing, as friendly professionals "not a school-teacher". Inside the app (blog images only) a small, unnamed, glossy **blue blob with two white eyes** stands for the AI tutor. It acts as a presence indicator ("Tutor speaking…") rather than a storyteller (`blog/speak-blog-winter-2025-tutor-lessons-ai-tutor-character.png`). 3D emoji (flame, taco) handle reward moments.
- **Illustration style:** **none.** The visual language is product UI on light-grey canvases: white lesson cards with a mint phrase highlight (`feature-practice-phrase-card`), chat bubbles marked "Perfect line" / "Could be improved" (`feature-apply-chat-feedback`), and a blue bar chart of phrases spoken per day. People are photographed or filmed. The only "graphic" is the logo: four rounded vertical pills read as a voice waveform, unchanged since at least 2019 (`blog/wayback-2020-usespeak-app-icon-256.png`).
- **Colour:** essentially **one colour**. Electric blue **`#1C49FF`** fills the logo mark, every CTA, a full-bleed band ("Don't just learn languages, start speaking them", `#3748F7`) and a giant rounded footer card with a white "Speak" wordmark. Navy `#12205A` is used for text; muted `#8990AD` makes **two-tone headlines** ("Three steps **to spoken fluency**", "Frequently **Asked Questions**"). Accents come only from feedback: mint `#29C195` for correct, tomato `#FF4242` for errors, gold stars. The background is white and `#F5F5F5`.
- **Shape and type:** **Axiforma** (geometric sans) at 56px/600 with **tight −0.04em tracking**, sentence case. Pretendard is used for Korean and Japanese. Buttons are **full pills** (`border-radius: 128px`, 62px tall, flat blue, no shadow, 600 weight) with a trailing arrow ("Get Started →"). Cards have large radii (~24px), and the nav is a floating white pill bar.
- **Voice:** confident, calm and adult: "Good things happen when we Speak", "Real conversation. No pressure.", "Repeat it until you feel confident". The CTO's posts talk about giving the learner "room to think" and not correcting every imperfection. Social proof comes from big numbers (4.8, 15M+ downloads, "1,110 phrases out loud" in week one) and awards (Apple App of the Day, Wirecutter).
- **Habit and reward (as marketed):** the "Speak Method" loop (Learn → Practice → Apply), a weekly phrases-spoken chart, and streaks in the app (blog).
- **Chinese / Cantonese specifics:** Chinese is listed among the course languages, and the footer links to 繁體中文 and 简体中文 sites. Nothing Cantonese-specific.
